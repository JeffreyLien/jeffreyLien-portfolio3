## Resume entry

**Institutional Research & Student Success Analytics | SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI**

- Built an end-to-end institutional research workflow across 25K synthetic students, integrating enrollment, course, advising, financial-aid, and student-feedback data to support persistence, academic, Student Affairs, and external-reporting decisions.
- Developed SQL reporting marts, automated data-quality and submission QA checks, cohort/persistence analyses, qualitative feedback summaries, and an Excel/Power BI reporting package to identify retention gaps, high-withdrawal courses, and student-support priorities.

## Interview positioning

This project is designed to demonstrate institutional-research workflow rather than predictive-model complexity: first validate source data, define reporting logic, build reproducible datasets, reconcile metrics, communicate findings, and preserve cautious interpretation.
