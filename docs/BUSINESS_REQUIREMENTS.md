# Business Requirements

## Stakeholders
- Director of Institutional Research
- Institutional Effectiveness
- Academic leadership
- Student Affairs / advising
- Program leaders
- External reporting entities

## Core decisions supported
1. Where is student persistence changing?
2. Which high-volume courses require academic review?
3. Which currently enrolled student groups may benefit from proactive support?
4. Are institutional reports complete, reconciled, and submission-ready?
5. What themes appear in qualitative student feedback?

## Data quality expectations
- Unique keys
- Standardized program values
- Valid credit ranges
- Reconciled headcounts
- Rates constrained to 0-100%
- Reproducible transformations
- Clear distinction between association and causation

## Confidentiality
All records in this repository are synthetic. In production, student-level data would be access-controlled and handled under FERPA/institutional privacy policies.
