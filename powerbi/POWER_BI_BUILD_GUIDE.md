# Power BI Build Guide

Load:
- `student_term_metrics.csv`
- `course_performance.csv`
- `cohort_retention.csv`
- `feedback_themes.csv`
- `external_submission_validated.csv`

## Page 1 — Institutional Overview
KPI cards: students, persistence, credit completion, high-risk student-terms.

## Page 2 — Retention & Cohorts
Line chart by entry cohort and relative term; filters for program, degree level, first-generation, transfer.

## Page 3 — Course Performance
Scatter of enrollment volume vs withdrawal rate; priority table with failure and credit-completion rates.

## Page 4 — Student Support & Feedback
Advising volume, resolution status, observed persistence by support status, feedback themes and satisfaction.

## Page 5 — External Reporting QA
Submission rows, QC pass status, reconciliation, and failed-check table.

Use descriptive language such as **observed** and **associated with**; avoid causal claims.
